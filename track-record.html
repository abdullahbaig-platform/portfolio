Methodology

Every piece on this site is built to be challenged, reproduced, and reviewed — not taken on faith. Assumptions are stated explicitly rather than buried in a spreadsheet, and the underlying model ships alongside every call so the numbers can be checked directly rather than argued about after the fact. When a thesis is wrong, it stays on this page.
