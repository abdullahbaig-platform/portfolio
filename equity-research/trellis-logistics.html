Trellis Logistics Corp (TRLS)

Avoid, PT $18 (12mo). Consensus is still modeling a freight-rate recovery into next year; capacity additions over the last two years suggest pricing power stays weak for longer than the Street expects.

Business overview

Trellis Logistics operates a truckload and brokerage network across regional freight lanes. Utilization and spot rates are both a function of industry-wide capacity, which expanded materially in the last up-cycle and has been slow to exit even as volumes softened.

Valuation

DCF assumptions (stated explicitly): revenue roughly flat over the next two years as rate pressure offsets modest volume growth, EBITDA margin compressing from 9.5% to 8.0% before stabilizing, WACC of 9.0%, terminal growth of 2.0%. This produces a base-case intrinsic value of roughly $18/share versus a current price near $24.

What would break this thesis

This view breaks if the spot rate index inflects upward for two consecutive months alongside falling active truck counts — that combination would signal capacity is exiting faster than modeled here.

Conclusion

Base case intrinsic value of $18 against a market still pricing in a rate recovery the capacity data doesn't yet support. Avoid / short, 12 month horizon.
