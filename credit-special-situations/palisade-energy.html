Palisade Energy — distressed debt situation overview

A borrowing-base redetermination has cut available liquidity sharply, handing effective control of the situation to the RBL lender group. Senior notes recovery now depends heavily on whether non-core assets sell for enough to bridge the gap.

Capital structure

TrancheAmountSecurity
Reserve-based lending (RBL) facility$340m drawn / $380m borrowing baseFirst lien on reserves
Senior unsecured notes$425mUnsecured
Total debt$765m

Why the company is under stress

A sustained pullback in commodity prices reduced the PV-9 value of proved reserves, triggering a borrowing-base redetermination that cut available RBL capacity. That leaves the company with thin liquidity headroom against near-term interest obligations, and the senior notes — sitting behind a fully secured, asset-backed facility — carry structural subordination risk on top of the commodity exposure.

Recovery waterfall

ScenarioAsset valueRBL recoverySenior notes recovery
Commodity price recovery$620m100%~66%
Prolonged downturn$410m100%~16%

The RBL is well-covered in both scenarios given its first-lien, asset-backed position; senior notes recovery is highly sensitive to the commodity path, which is the crux of the trade.

Likely restructuring path

The RBL lender group is well-positioned to dictate terms given its senior secured position and control over incremental liquidity. Expect asset sales of non-core acreage to raise cash ahead of any broader restructuring, with senior notes likely negotiating for an equitized recovery or extended maturity rather than a full paydown, given the coverage gap in the downside scenario.

What would break this thesis

This view breaks if the next borrowing-base redetermination is held flat or increased — that would signal reserve values have stabilized and materially improve the senior notes' recovery profile in the downside case.

Conclusion

RBL lenders are well covered; senior notes are the security to watch, with recovery bifurcated almost entirely by the commodity price path over the next 12 months.
