Meridian Foods Inc. (MERI)

Long, PT $54 (12–18mo). Gross margin has bottomed as input-cost inflation rolls off, and the market is still pricing Meridian as a low-growth commodity packager rather than crediting the private-label volume it has quietly picked up over the last two years.

Business overview

Meridian Foods is a mid-cap packaged foods company selling shelf-stable and frozen goods across branded and private-label lines. Roughly a third of volume now runs through retailer private-label programs, up from a fifth three years ago — a mix shift that carries lower unit economics but has been the company's only reliable growth lever while branded volume has stagnated.

Valuation

DCF assumptions (stated explicitly): revenue growth of 4.0% in year one, stepping down to a 2.5% terminal rate by year five; EBITDA margin expanding from 11.0% currently to 13.5% by year five as input costs normalize; WACC of 8.5%; terminal growth of 2.5%. This produces a base-case intrinsic value of roughly $54/share.

What would break this thesis

This view breaks if gross margin fails to expand past 34% for two consecutive quarters, or if a top-three retail customer signals it is re-bidding its private-label contract — either would indicate the mix shift isn't sticking.

Conclusion

Base case intrinsic value of $54 against a market price still anchored to Meridian's weaker branded-only history. Long, 12–18 month horizon.
