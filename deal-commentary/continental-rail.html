Continental Rail explores sale of its intermodal unit

Deal facts

SellerContinental Rail (parent)
AssetIntermodal terminals & drayage unit
Process stageEarly — advisors reportedly engaged, no price disclosed
Unit revenue (est.)~$640m LTM
Announced9 Aug 2026

Stated rationale vs. the actual read

Stated: Continental Rail describes this as "sharpening focus on core network operations."

Actual read: the parent's net debt/EBITDA has drifted up over the last two years and this looks primarily like a deleveraging move — the intermodal unit is a clean, separable asset with its own customer base, which makes it a logical piece to sell rather than a genuine strategic pivot away from it.

Valuation check

Comparable intermodal/drayage unit sales over the last three years have priced between 7.5x and 8.5x EBITDA. Applying that range to an estimated ~$95m unit EBITDA implies a sale price of roughly $710m–$810m — enough to meaningfully move parent leverage but not transformative on its own.

Likely financing structure and leverage implications

A cash sale applied to debt paydown would bring parent net debt/EBITDA down by an estimated 0.3–0.4 turns at the midpoint of the valuation range above — modest but directionally supportive of the stated strategic rationale, even if deleveraging looks like the primary driver rather than portfolio focus.

What to watch next

Whether a strategic buyer (another rail or logistics operator) or a financial sponsor emerges as the lead bidder — a sponsor buyer would suggest the asset was priced for a leveraged return rather than strategic fit; and whether Continental Rail's next earnings call confirms use-of-proceeds as debt paydown versus reinvestment.
